# Snuzzle (working name)

The app shows the name Snuzzle to users. The project, folders and code still use the earlier name Alji;
rename those only once the name is final.

A screen-free bedtime ritual app for families with eczema. The parent massages moisturizer in,
guided by a calm voice and spa music, then the child drifts off to a story. Native iOS, SwiftUI,
StoreKit 2. No third-party SDKs.

## Run it (Mac + Xcode 15 or newer)
```bash
brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate
open Alji.xcodeproj
```
Pick an iPhone simulator and press Run. Purchases work locally through `Alji/Resources/Products.storekit`
(no App Store account needed for testing). Reset test purchases in Xcode: Debug › StoreKit › Manage Transactions.

> This code was written in a Linux environment without Xcode, so it has not been compiled yet.
> Expect a few small fixes on the first build.

## How the app is laid out
```
Packages/AljiDesignSystem   Design system: one font (Figtree, 12 styles), colors, spacing, onboarding, paywall and main-app parts
Alji/App                    App entry + root flow
Alji/Onboarding             The funnel, built screen for screen from the approved prototype
                            (OnboardingScript = wording and logic, OnboardingBlocks = visuals,
                            OnboardingArtData = illustrations generated by scripts/generate-onboarding-art.py)
Alji/Paywall                Paywall, second offer, welcome-after-purchase
Alji/Main                   Tonight, massage player, Stories, audio player, Create a story, Progress, settings
                            (MassageStepFigure = the hands and strokes, MassageArtData = body, foot and palm
                            drawings generated by scripts/generate-massage-art.py)
Alji/Models                 Onboarding answers, the story library (StoryCatalogData, generated from the
                            prototype), nights logged (AppState), progress maths (ProgressStats)
Alji/Services               StoreKit, story voice, sleepy music, white and pink noise, reminders
Alji/Config/AppConfig.swift Everything to change before launch
scripts/check-fonts.sh          Fails if any screen styles fonts outside the design system
```

## The main app
Built screen for screen from the approved prototype. Three tabs:
- **Tonight**: tonight's story as a picture-book banner and one button. "How's the skin tonight?" (Calm → tonight's
  massage story, Flaring → the shorter gentle story), then the follow-along player: each step opens with a short
  "Preview" (the timer pauses, the hands show the move, the voice says what to do), then "Your turn" with the story
  and a scene from it behind the figure. At the end a star lands and affirmations start by themselves (off,
  20 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours); nothing is asked at night and the screen goes dark. Tonight's story changes every
  three nights.
- **The next morning**: one notification, and the check-in opens as its own screen over the home screen: three quick
  questions plus a story rating, then a result screen with a small reward every day. "Later" leaves a button on Tonight.
- **Stories**: shelves of picture-book covers: massage stories, audio-only bedtime stories (5, 10 or 20 minutes),
  affirmations, calm moments, favorites, and Create a story.
- **Progress**: the child's sky (every finished night is a star; five make a constellation; color shows calm, itchy
  and flare nights), time back for you, itch trend, rings, milestones, totals, research. Every number comes from the
  family's own check-ins; sections show a short note until there is enough data.

Story covers, the banner and the massage scenes are placeholder drawings generated from the prototype by
`scripts/generate-story-art.py` (`Alji/Main/StoryArtData.swift`). Swap in final art in `Alji/Main/StoryArtViews.swift`.

Free version: `StoryCatalog.freeIDs` in `Alji/Main/MainRouter.swift`. Everything else opens the paywall.

## The funnel
Promise → founder story (mom's massage, glow-star ceiling) → child's name → age → your relationship
(Lumi's stories then say "Mommy", "Grandpa"…) → your name → eczema areas → then question → cited insight:
night waking → "7 in 10 kids" · cream time → "it's not you" · touch response → adapted plan ·
AAD + 1998 study on massage · bedtime length, parent sleep, feelings → "it helps you too" (2023 trials) ·
what you've tried · goals → struggle score → transformation timeline → features → reminder →
building the plan → custom plan → **1-minute taste of Lumi's massage story** → first win → paywall.
A glowing star travels the progress bar.

Safety rule from the AAD: no massage on actively flaring skin. Flaring areas get gentle smoothing only.

Paywall rules: the headline remembers the goal they picked; yearly preselected and shown per month;
3-day free trial on yearly only; an honest timeline with the real price and charge date; trial reminder
on by default; visible close; "no" opens a cheaper second offer, then the free version
(5-minute ritual, first story, two core techniques). Set `AppConfig.paywallIsHard = true` for an
unskippable paywall instead.

Pricing: $59.99/year (3-day free trial), $9.99/month, second offer $34.99/year.

- [ ] Real Terms of Use and Privacy Policy URLs, and a support email
- [ ] Rewrite the founder note in your own words; add real beta reviews only, with permission
- [ ] Create a story uses a built-in template (`StoryCatalog.customStory`); connect an AI writing service

- [ ] Replace the device voice with recorded narration and the generated pad with licensed spa music
- [ ] Have a dermatologist or pediatric massage professional review the technique scripts
- [ ] App Store privacy labels: data is stored only on the device (no accounts, no analytics yet)
- [ ] Test the full flow in TestFlight, including buying, cancelling, restoring and the free version

## Evidence used in the app
- Eczema Society of Canada caregiver survey (361 families): sleep disturbance in 70% of children with
  eczema and 55% of caregivers.
- Schachner, Field et al., *Pediatric Dermatology* 1998: children with eczema whose parents massaged their
  cream in for 20 minutes nightly for a month improved more than controls; parents' anxiety dropped.
- Rezaei et al., 2023 randomized trial (140 infants): a 15-minute bedtime massage improved sleep onset,
  night wakings and longest sleep stretch; mothers slept longer.
